# Stage 3 settlement-space policy

User-authorized chain-role and tier policy. Each value is a cumulative installed-level percentage-point contribution to the existing Stage 2 infrastructure-space accumulator; upgrades replace earlier tiers. No condition score or native-output metadata is converted into space.

Housing: +10 per tier, cottages +12, dense tenements +20. Supporting drainage/water infrastructure: +1 to +4. Intensive footprints: -0.5 or -1. Mines and small/non-residential services: zero. These are authored implementation balance values under the user decision, not recovered historical values. A housing tier outweighs any individual footprint penalty by at least tenfold; four maximum-tier intensive chains (-32 total) remain below one eight-tier cottage chain (+96). Existing Stage 2 capacity clamps and condition factors remain authoritative.
